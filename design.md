# Portfolio design direction

## Purpose
A professional home for Raashid Arquil's software work, independent products, and freelance services. Visitors should reach every selected project in one click and understand its current maturity.

## Editorial source
Assignment-Answers.md is the source for personal stories and new project copy. Do not publish invented client relationships, adoption statistics, measured results, testimonials, or contributions. Existing AI-authored claims need verification before reuse.

## Visual direction
Warm paper and ink, copper accents, Manrope body type, Instrument Serif display accents. Large product covers and deliberate spacing carry the page. Avoid repeated nested cards, technology logo walls, glows, pulsing availability badges, and decorative animation.

People and imagery: no illustrations of living beings. Raashid's portrait is permitted. Other people's faces require permission; silhouettes are acceptable. Prefer no emojis. Product covers use typography and abstract geometry; they are not screenshots.

## Page jobs
- Home: introduce the engineer, show KeepClose and CropCare, expose services and selected experiences.
- Work: selected products and prototypes, with earlier work in a secondary archive.
- Project pages: origin, contribution, working state, limitations, and next steps; implementation detail only where substantiated.
- Services: three scoped offers and the proposed working process, without suggesting an established client track record.
- About: voice, background, AI-assisted working method, and actual participation.
- Contact: email and optional enquiry prompts, plus employment and collaboration routes.

## Implementation
Astro static output and existing content collections. Shared ProductCover and ContactBanner components. Theme persistence is optional and must tolerate storage failures. Native disclosure mobile menu works without JavaScript. Skip link and visible focus indicators are required. Static project galleries link to full-size images without modal focus problems.

## Before publishing
Visually review desktop/mobile, light/dark, font loading, focus order, and menu behavior. Add only approved event images. Check dates and hackathon category. Keep product statuses honest. Deployment requires a separate user instruction.
