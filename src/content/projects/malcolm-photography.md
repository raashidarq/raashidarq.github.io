## Overview

Designed and built as a bespoke web presence for a photography business. The primary focus was delivering fast image delivery, intuitive gallery navigation, and seamless booking inquiry management.

---

## Engineering Learnings

- **Asset Compression & Lazy Loading**: Working with high-resolution wildlife photography underscored the critical need for responsive image breakpoints and progressive JPEG loading to avoid network bottlenecks.
- **Form Sanitization**: Implemented server-side input validation and CSRF protection in PHP for client contact and booking submissions.
