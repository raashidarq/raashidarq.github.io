A while back, I joined Ascentic's AI Launchpad excited about what I could learn. Eventually, I found myself presenting on stage at TRACE, Sri Lanka.

I built **CropCare**, an offline-first mobile app designed to help smallholder farmers identify possible crop pests and diseases, get relevant advice, and refer cases to agricultural experts through WhatsApp.

Through the programme, CropCare went from an early idea to a working MVP, and eventually made the Top 20, and later the Top 10.

## Start with the problem

For me, one of the biggest takeaways was learning to start with the problem rather than the technology. It's easy as a developer to find an interesting technology and then look for something to build with it.

The programme pushed me to first understand who has the problem, why it exists, what solutions already exist, and what a useful solution might actually look like. AI then became a tool to help build that solution, not the reason for building it.

## Moving deliberately

I also learned that moving quickly and moving deliberately aren't the same thing. AI can help you move very quickly, but that makes it even more important to understand what you're building, the decisions you're making, and the trade-offs involved.
