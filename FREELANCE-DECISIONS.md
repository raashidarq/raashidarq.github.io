# Decide what freelance work to offer

Leave the current services copy unchanged while answering these. Use real examples and constraints; “not sure yet” is a useful answer.

1. Which would you most want to deliver first: a business website, a WordPress/Elementor site, a dashboard or integration, or a mobile prototype? Rank your top two and say why.
2. For each choice, what have you already built that demonstrates you can deliver it? Which parts could you explain, debug, and maintain independently when AI output is wrong?
3. Which client and problem can you describe concretely? For example, a travel business needing enquiries, an individual launching a product, or a small team replacing a spreadsheet. What would they pay to improve?
4. What is the smallest useful paid deliverable you could complete for that client? List what is included and three things excluded.
5. What must the client provide before you start: copy, photographs, branding, designs, hosting access, existing code, or a decision-maker? Which of these would you also offer to handle?
6. How many hours per week can you reliably allocate around study and your apps? How many simultaneous client projects would fit without abandoning those commitments?
7. Which responsibilities are you comfortable owning after handover: deployment, updates, backups, monitoring, fixes, or ongoing support? Which require a separate agreement or another specialist?
8. What payment structure would make you comfortable starting: a fixed small scope with milestones, hourly work with a cap, or a paid discovery stage? What would make you decline an unpaid trial?
9. When a client adds a feature, who decides the priority, and how would you explain the effect on cost and timing? Write a short example reply in your own voice.
10. How do you want to meet your first clients: people you know, local businesses, referrals, platforms, or public demonstrations? Which route could you act on this week?
11. What kinds of work or clients would you decline, even if paid? Include ethical boundaries, unclear ownership, rushed timelines, missing access, and skills you cannot yet support.
12. What outcome would make your first three engagements successful besides income: a repeat client, a usable public case study, delivery experience, or a specific technical skill? Rank these.

Start by answering 1, 2, 4, 6, and 7. Those determine a responsible initial offer. The other questions shape customer handling, pricing discussions, and how you find work.
