CropCare
Apparently when it came to projects, I had been thinking too small - I thought what can little old me do to problems that affect that many people?

I had joined this Launchpad program, where the people in the program got us to look for a problem and idea to solve in the program - they'd teach us how to use the tools and some theoretical frameworks to use to get a robust real-world solution. 

So we got to work.

My dad threw a couple ideas that I could work on, at least look into. So we decided agriculture, agriculture is pretty big in Sri Lanka and around the world. I was skeptical, but ...okay, I'll do my research. After some digging, I found problems that 'hey? I could build something that answers these issues couldn't I? why not, lets do it!'

So I learnt what I needed using YouTube and whatever else available, make sure I understand what I'm building and why its needed for the build, decide the pros and cons of a feature/implementation, consider non-functional requirements, designed the user flow and system diagrams, and used AI to implement.

The app uses on-device ML to classify pest/diseases on plants by outputting a set of probabilities. Uses Shanon Entropy and a confidence threshold, to mitigate accuracy. Then set the end 'diagnosis' results to an AI model, alongside other information, to interpret and respond in structured format. 

Now it works functionally, real world? could do better- hence why I haven't put it out to Playstore/ set it out as a product to actively use...the accuracy of the ML model can be better, the treatment guidance can have better guardrails and stuff like that. 

I have really nice pictures of the event - Launchpad had us present to an audience at TRACE city Sri Lanka. Quite the experience. I also have a 1 minute video which I feel could be better but is not bad. And screenshots - which can be shared. I need high quality mockups, launchposts, and the like.
